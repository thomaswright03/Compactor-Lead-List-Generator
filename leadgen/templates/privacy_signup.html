This policy explains what Lead Finder (the "service") keeps and why. Wright AI Solutions ("we", "us") sells the service to companies (each a "customer") and runs it for them. Questions: {{ contact }}.

Whose data it is

Each customer owns the data its users put in the service. We process it on the customer's behalf, only to provide, secure and support the service, and never sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with another customer. Each customer's leads, marks, calls and notes are kept separate from every other customer's, and a user sees only their own company's.

Your current customer list

The Current customers page lets a customer keep businesses it already services off its lead lists. It is built so that neither we nor the service ever receives the list:

  • The file you pick (Excel or CSV) is read only in your own browser. It is never uploaded, and its customers' names, phone numbers and addresses never leave your computer.
  • Your browser turns each customer's phone number, street address and business name into a one-way fingerprint (a SHA-256 code salted with a value unique to your company) and sends only those fingerprints.
  • The service stores only the fingerprints, the number of customers on the list, when it was put on file, and the name of whoever did it. It compares them with fingerprints made from your company's own saved leads to hide matching businesses.
  • A fingerprint can't be turned back into a customer's details. Someone holding the fingerprints could still test a guess, for example a phone number they already suspect, so the service treats the fingerprints as confidential and keeps them only in its database and its encrypted backups.
  • Uploading a new list replaces the old fingerprints. Clear the customer list deletes them from the live database at once; copies in backups expire with those backups (below).

What else the service keeps

  • Your account: your company's name, and each user's name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes, never as typed.
  • Billing: your plan, its billing period and payment history. Card payments are handled by our payment processor; the service never sees or stores your full card number.
  • Business leads: public information about businesses (name, address, phone, website, category, map position) from OpenStreetMap and Google Places, with the score the service gives each one.
  • Your team's work: Yes / No answers, call summaries and outcomes, verified phone numbers and contact names, and searches, each with who saved it and when.
  • Login: a signed cookie keeps you logged in for up to {{ login_days }} days. Wrong passwords are counted for a short time to stop guessing.
  • In your browser: your light or dark choice, unsent call notes and whether you have seen the tutorial are kept in this browser's local storage. The service uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.
  • Problems: errors and failed searches are recorded so they can be fixed. These reports hold plain messages, not your data.

Where it is kept and who sees it

The service runs on Render, its database is on Neon (United States), and a nightly copy of the database is encrypted (AES-256) and kept for 90 days. Pages and data travel only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Only your company's users, and our staff to run and support the service, can see your company's data. We share data only with the providers that host and bill for the service, for that purpose, or where the law requires it.

Where the leads come from

Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors. Business details from Google Places are used under Google's terms, and Google's own Privacy Policy covers what Google receives when the service asks it for them (the area searched, never your customer data).

How long and your choices

Your company's data is kept while it subscribes. Its account owner can download its leads at any time and can ask {{ contact }} to export all of its data or delete it. When an account closes, its data is deleted after 30 days. Deleted data leaves the encrypted backups within 90 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and customers will be told by email of any change that matters to them.
