How it works

Three steps from an empty call sheet to a list worth working.

  1. Set your area

    Enter your shop's address and how far you go, up to {{ area_miles }} miles.

  2. Find leads

    Lead Finder searches map and business listings for grocery stores, warehouses, hospitals, plants and every other kind of business likely to run a baler or compactor, and scores each one.

  3. Work the list

    Call the strongest leads first, mark who has equipment, log every call, and download the list to Excel or CSV whenever you like.

What you get

One shared list for your whole sales team.

Scored leads

Every business gets a score from 0 to 100 for how likely it is to use a baler or compactor, with the reasons written out, so your team calls the best ones first.

A map of your area

See every lead on a map around your shop, colored by whether it has been checked yet, to plan a day of visits or calls by neighborhood.

Yes / No checking

Mark whether each business has a baler or compactor. Everyone on your team sees the answer, so no one calls the same place twice.

A log of every call

Record each call's outcome and notes, verified phone numbers and contact names, and see who needs a follow-up.

Your customers stay private
