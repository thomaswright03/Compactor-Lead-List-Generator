This policy explains what the {{ company }} Lead Finder (the "site") keeps and why. Wright AI Solutions builds and runs the site for {{ company }} (the "company"). Questions: {{ contact }}.

Your current customer list

The Current customers page lets the company keep businesses it already services off the lead lists. It is built so that neither Wright AI Solutions nor the site ever receives the list:

  • The file you pick (Excel or CSV) is read only in your own browser. It is never uploaded, and its customers' names, phone numbers and addresses never leave your computer.
  • Your browser turns each customer's phone number, street address and business name into a one-way fingerprint (a SHA-256 code made with a value unique to this site) and sends only those fingerprints.
  • The site stores only the fingerprints, the number of customers on the list, when it was put on file, and the name of whoever did it. It compares them with fingerprints made from its own saved leads to hide matching businesses.
  • A fingerprint can't be turned back into a customer's details. Someone holding the fingerprints could still test a guess, for example a phone number they already suspect, so the site treats the fingerprints as confidential and keeps them only in its database and its encrypted backups.
  • Uploading a new list replaces the old fingerprints. Clear the customer list deletes them from the live database at once; copies in backups expire with those backups (below).

What else the site keeps

  • Business leads: public information about businesses (name, address, phone, website, category, map position) from Yelp and OpenStreetMap, with the score the site gives each one.
  • Your team's work: Yes / No answers, call summaries and outcomes, verified phone numbers and contact names, and searches, each with the name typed in "Your name" and when it was saved.
  • Login: a signed cookie keeps you logged in for up to {{ login_days }} days. Wrong passwords are counted by network address for a short time to stop guessing; that count is kept in memory only.
  • In your browser: your name, light or dark choice, unsent call notes and whether you have seen the tutorial are kept in this browser's local storage and never sent anywhere else. The site uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.
  • Problems: errors and failed searches are recorded so they can be fixed, and may be emailed to Wright AI Solutions. These reports hold plain messages, not your data.

Where it is kept and who sees it

The site runs on Render, its database is on Neon (United States), and a nightly copy of the database is encrypted (AES-256) and kept for 90 days as a GitHub Actions artifact. Only people with the site's login, and Wright AI Solutions to run and support it, can see the data. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long and your choices

Saved leads and the team's work are kept while the company uses the site. The company can ask {{ contact }} to export or delete its data. Deleted data leaves the encrypted backups within 90 days.
